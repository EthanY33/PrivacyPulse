About This Extension

Cookie Consent Guardian is a privacy-focused browser extension that helps you understand and control how websites track you. Instead of blindly clicking "Accept All" on cookie banners, this extension shows you exactly what you're agreeing to.

Key Features

Cookie Banner Interception

Automatically detects and intercepts cookie consent banners

Privacy Score Analysis

Get a clear score (0-100) showing how private each site is

Tracker Detection

Identifies analytics, advertising, and social media trackers

Raw Data Viewer

See all cookies, localStorage, and sessionStorage data

Your Privacy: This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any of your browsing data. All analysis happens locally in your browser.
