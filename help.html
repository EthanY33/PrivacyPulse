Learn how to use Cookie Consent Guardian effectively

Getting Started

Cookie Consent Guardian automatically protects your privacy by intercepting cookie consent banners and showing you exactly what data websites want to collect before you agree.

Automatic Protection

The extension automatically detects and intercepts cookie banners on websites you visit.

Privacy Analysis

View detailed breakdowns of cookies, trackers, and data collection practices.

Raw Site Data

See all cookies, localStorage, and sessionStorage data in a clean table format.

Remember Preferences

Your choices are saved per-site so you don't have to decide every time.

Understanding Privacy Scores

Each website receives a privacy score from 0-100 based on its tracking practices:

85
Good Privacy (75-100)

Minimal tracking, mostly essential cookies

55
Moderate Privacy (50-74)

Some analytics and third-party cookies present

25
Poor Privacy (0-49)

Heavy tracking, advertising cookies, multiple trackers

Tip: Click "How is this calculated?" in the modal to see exactly what factors affect the score.

How to Use

Viewing Site Data

Click the extension icon in your browser toolbar, then click "View Site Data" to see a complete privacy analysis of the current website, including:

  • Privacy Analysis tab: Cookie categories, trackers, and data collection practices
  • Raw Site Data tab: All cookies, localStorage, and sessionStorage in table format

When a Cookie Banner Appears

When you click "Accept" on a cookie banner, the extension intercepts and shows you:

  • The privacy score for the site
  • How many cookies will be set (by category)
  • What trackers are present
  • What data will be collected

You can then choose to:

  • Reject All: Block non-essential cookies
  • Customize: Open the site's cookie settings
  • Accept Anyway: Proceed with accepting all cookies

Managing Preferences

  • Toggle Protection: Enable/disable the extension from the popup
  • Clear Site Preference: Reset your saved choice for the current site
  • Clear All Preferences: Reset all saved choices for all sites

FAQ

Why don't I see the warning on some sites?

The extension only intercepts known cookie banner patterns. Some sites use custom implementations that may not be detected. You can still view site data by clicking the extension icon.

Does this block all cookies?

No. The extension educates you about cookies but doesn't automatically block them. When you choose "Reject All," it attempts to click the site's reject button or clear non-essential cookies.

Why is my preference not remembered?

Preferences are stored locally in your browser. If you clear your browser data or use incognito mode, preferences won't persist.

What's the difference between cookies and trackers?

Cookies are small files stored by websites. Trackers are scripts that collect data about your behavior, often using cookies. A site can have cookies without trackers, but most trackers use cookies.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. All analysis happens locally in your browser. The extension doesn't collect or transmit any of your browsing data.

Troubleshooting

Extension not working? Try reloading the page or restarting Chrome. Some sites require a fresh page load for the extension to detect their cookie banners.
  • Modal not appearing: The site may use a cookie banner that isn't recognized. Use "View Site Data" from the popup instead.
  • Errors in console: Try reloading the extension from chrome://extensions and refreshing the page.
  • Stats not updating: Statistics are saved periodically. They should persist across browser restarts.
